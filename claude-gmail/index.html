Claude Gmail personal

Claude Gmail personal is a private helper that lets Christian Holden's AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) work with his own Gmail account on his own computer. It is not offered to the public and has one user: Christian.

What it does

  • Searches and reads Christian's email and attachments when he asks.
  • Creates draft replies for him to review.

It cannot send, delete, archive or relabel email. Christian reviews and sends every draft himself from Gmail.

How it works

The helper runs only on Christian's personal computer, using the open-source Google Workspace MCP server. It connects to Gmail through Google's official Gmail API after Christian signs in with Google and approves access. There is no website, server or database behind it.
