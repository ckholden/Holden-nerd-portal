How to update the app

Help

Listening

  • Tap Listen to start. Keep HoldenDMR open on your screen while you listen.
  • The buttons at the top of Listen switch networks. Now playing shows who is talking, or who talked last.
  • HOLDEN DMR is our private family channel; HOLDEN1 is the TGIF family channel.
  • No sound? Turn up the Volume slider and your phone's volume. Check that the top of the screen says Connected. If it says Reconnecting…, wait: it comes back by itself.
  • Done? Tap Stop listening.

Your talk group

Hold the button to talk to the family. It never goes out on a radio.

  • Green Hold to talk: press and hold, talk, then let go to listen.
  • Amber Channel busy: someone else is talking. Wait for them, or press twice to talk over.
  • Grey: you can't talk right now. The button says why (for example Reconnecting…).
  • The first time, your phone asks to use the microphone. Tap Allow. One press can last up to 2 minutes.

Texts & alerts

  • Call alerts send a notification when someone starts talking. Turn them on in the Texts tab. Notifications must be on too (Settings).
  • Radio texts (if Christian turned them on for you): pick who it goes to, type, and tap Send. Texts go out over amateur radio, so keep them plain: no business, no codes, no bad language.
  • A red number on the Texts tab means new texts.

Settings

  • Turn on notifications so alerts reach this phone.
  • Speaker picks where the sound plays (on computers).
  • Stop listening turns the radio off. Sign out signs you out on this device.

Updating the app

HoldenDMR updates itself every time you open it. If you see the blue New HoldenDMR app bar, tap How to update and follow the steps. On iPhone you remove the old icon and add the app to your home screen again.
