Help

Talking

  • Press and hold the big green HOLD TO TALK button. Wait for the short beep, then talk. Let go to listen.
  • OPENING MIC… means the phone is turning the microphone on. Keep holding; it only takes a moment.
  • The button turns red while you are on the air and shows how long you have been talking.
  • CHANNEL BUSY (yellow) means someone else is talking. Wait for them to finish. If you really must talk over them, press twice.
  • Talking stops by itself after 2 minutes (the time-out). You can change that in Settings.
  • Talking also stops if you switch to another tab, lock the phone, or the network drops. Press again when you are ready.
  • Let go before you change channel or network; the app won't switch while you are talking.
  • Prefer to tap instead of hold? Settings → Push-to-talk mode → Tap to toggle. Then it says TAP TO TALK and TAP TO STOP.
  • On a computer, click and hold the talk button the same way. The Space bar does nothing until you turn on Settings → Space bar talks on that computer; then hold Space for half a second to talk. That keeps typing, dictation and foot pedals from keying the radio.

Channels & networks

  • The row of buttons at the top picks the network (HOLDEN, TGIF, BM = BrandMeister, PNW, BC). The big card shows the channel you are on.
  • HOLDEN DMR is our private family channel; HOLDEN1 is the TGIF family channel.
  • Tap a favourite button to change channel. Tap ☆ on the big card to add the channel you are on to your favourites.
  • All talkgroups lets you search every channel on that network. Leave TG goes quiet.
  • 📟 PAGE is in Texts → Command Line, under Family radios (on HOLDEN1 or HOLDEN DMR, if your account can page): tap a name to send them an alert. Add an optional note first, like “come up on OR1”.
  • BrandMeister channels need your own BrandMeister account: Settings → BRANDMEISTER, enter your Hotspot Security password and tap Enable.
  • A yellow PATCHED banner means HOLDEN DMR is linked to TGIF HOLDEN1 for a while, so what you say is heard on both.

Command Line

  • Command Line is at the top of the Texts tab. Type commands there; anything that isn't a command goes to the room chat, the same as the desktop app: everyone signed in to HoldenDMR sees what you type. Nothing in chat goes over the air.
  • Type help for the list of commands. A few: radios (every family radio, nothing transmitted), ping kk7ion, st (your status), lh (last heard), nr (net report), !kk7ion (look up a callsign), c t33611 (tune a talkgroup), dc (go quiet).
  • rc kk7ion and rollcall send a radio check over the air, so the app asks “are you sure” first. Nothing ever starts one by itself.
  • Family radios above the chat shows each radio: green = heard or answered in the last 15 minutes, yellow = hotspot on but quiet, grey = hotspot off. Ping transmits nothing.
  • Map, track, radar, aprs, @sms, @aprs, patch and bc are in the desktop app only.

Texts

  • Radio texts go to a ham's radio. In To, put a callsign (like KK7ION), a DMR ID, or holden for the family group.
  • A red number on the Texts tab means new texts. It clears when you open Texts.
  • Reply under a text fills in who to send it to.
  • Clear texts clears them on all your devices (a copy stays in the server log).
  • Texts are plain messages, not commands. Type commands like ping, rc, lh or st in the Command Line instead (the Command Line button at the top of Texts).
  • Radio Bulletins (in Settings) lists the latest news and notices for our radios.

Heard

Three views, picked at the top of the Heard tab:

  • Recent: everyone who has talked on your channel since you opened the app, newest first.
  • Last Heard: everyone heard on PNWDigital, on a handful of BrandMeister talkgroups (Oregon, PNW Regional, Worldwide and a few more), on the TGIF talkgroups in this app (with HOLDEN1 as our own bridge hears it) and on the KJ7DTS hotspot, with who is talking right now. The All, PNW, BM (BrandMeister), TGIF and Hotspot buttons pick which to show. Tap a talkgroup near the top to show only that one. Times are Pacific.
  • Map: the same people, each shown at the repeater they used (or their home town, state or country when that is all that is known). People with no known place aren't shown. Tap a dot to see who it is. Bigger dots are more people; brighter is more recent.

A Listen button appears under people heard on BrandMeister, in Last Heard and on the map. It moves this app to the BrandMeister talkgroup that person was heard on, and your talk button then talks there too. To go back: on the Talk tab, tap your usual network at the top (HOLDEN or TGIF), then your usual channel.

Last Heard and Map work even when monitoring is off, and update every 15 seconds while you are looking at them. If the screen isn't tapped or clicked for 10 minutes, the updates pause to save data; tap or click anywhere to start them again.

Alerts

  • Call alerts → Enable lets this phone get notifications. Do it once on each phone.
  • Key-up alerts tell you when someone starts talking on HOLDEN DMR, PRESTON'S TALK GROUP or TGIF HOLDEN1. Pick who (Anyone, family buttons, or any callsign or radio ID you add) and where. A callsign you add can only alert you when it talks in one of those places. A callsign matches every radio ID registered to it. When the radio only shows a callsign, a radio ID you added matches that person.
  • Nothing comes while you're talking, while you're listening there in the app, or for Radio, Parrot and our other machines. While the app is open in front of you, the alert shows at the top of the screen instead of as a notification.
  • Away alerts push you when someone talks on TGIF HOLDEN1 and none of your devices is connected. Turning it off does not turn off Key-up alerts.
  • iPhone: alerts only work after you add the app to your Home Screen and open it from there.

If something's wrong

  • Yellow “Reconnecting…” banner: the app lost its connection and is retrying by itself. You can't talk until it clears. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • “Network still connecting”: switching networks takes up to about 20 seconds. Wait, then try again.
  • Can't hear anything: check the Volume slider, your phone's volume, and Settings → Speaker.
  • Audio settings: Microphone and Speaker pick the device. Headset mode is louder but turns off echo cancelling, so use it only with headphones. Mic level sets how loud you sound to others.
  • “Microphone blocked”: allow the microphone for holdenportal.com. iPhone: Settings → Safari → Microphone. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Microphone.
  • Still stuck: close the app completely and open it again. If that doesn't fix it, tell Christian.
