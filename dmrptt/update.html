How to update to the new HoldenDMR app

The app has a new name and icon. Your phone may still show the old one (DMR PTT). Re-adding it takes about a minute.

Back to the app

iPhone or iPad

  1. Press and hold the old DMR PTT icon until a menu appears, then choose the option to remove or delete it.
  2. Open Safari and go to holdenportal.com/dmrptt/?app=hd2 (type all of it, including the ?app=hd2 on the end).
  3. Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open HoldenDMR from the home screen and sign in. Tap Connect and allow the microphone. Then tap Enable alerts and allow notifications.

Android

  • Chrome usually swaps the name and icon by itself within a day. You may not need to do anything.
  • If the icon still says the old name after a day: press and hold it, choose Uninstall or Remove, open Chrome, go to holdenportal.com/dmrptt, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). Then sign in and allow notifications.

PC desktop app

Nothing to do. It updates itself; if it shows Restart now, click it.

Your account and texts come back when you sign in. Favourites and settings on this phone (volume, microphone, time-out) may start fresh.
