Link control
 
⚠ "Link ON" puts strangers on the air. Anyone who connects to KJ7DTS-L is retransmitted on HOLDEN1, which KK7ION and KK7JVI also monitor, and you are the control operator for everything it sends. It asks before it sends, every time. This toggles the audio bridge only — the node stays online and connectable either way.
