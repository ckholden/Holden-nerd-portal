About this data

The question, answer, and rule-citation text in this app is derived from russolsen/ham_radio_question_pool, licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. That project transcribes the NCVEC (National Conference of Volunteer Examiner Coordinators) public-domain amateur radio license exam question pools into machine-readable form. It adds no explanations or commentary of its own — the plain-language explanations shown after each question in this app are original content written for this app, not part of the upstream source.

Technician (Element 2): 2026–2030 cycle, 409 questions. General (Element 3): 2023–2027 cycle, 423 questions. Extra (Element 4): 2024–2028 cycle, 599 questions.
