Peers

A peer is a device logged into the master: your hotspot, the DMR PTT app, the parrot, and the patch relay while a patch is on. Green means the master has not counted a missed keepalive for it. HBlink4 counts a miss after 30 s of silence (checked every 30 s) and drops the peer after 3, so a dead device turns yellow within about a minute and disappears within about two. This list is only live while the master's core is up; if the banner says the core is down, these rows are the last known state. An UNKNOWN peer is logged in but is not in the peer list on .151: either that list is out of date, or a device ID's passphrase has leaked.

Management

Talkgroups, devices, the HOLDEN1 patch, the recorded announcements (parrot intro and outro, patch ON and OFF), master restarts, log lines and the audit trail are on the Manage tab. HBlink4 has no runtime control API, so any change that touches the master's config restarts it, which drops every device for about 10 seconds. Edits are batched so that one Apply means at most one restart. The patch turns on and off without a restart.

Patch to TGIF

Private by default. A patch links one private talkgroup to the same TGIF talkgroup for a limited time. It is a separate relay process, so turning it on or off never restarts the master. Both sides hear a recorded ON announcement before anything is relayed and an OFF announcement at the end. Voice only; data and private calls never cross. A master restart, a VPS reboot or a lost TGIF login ends it. The ON/OFF clips are also listed under Announcements below.

Announcements recorded clips on the HOLDEN talkgroups

Loading…
Every clip is recorded from your radio. Press Record, then key up on that talkgroup before the countdown runs out and speak. The next over from an allowed radio ID becomes the clip and replaces the old one; the parrot plays a new clip back once so you hear it. Any other over is echoed as usual and the recording stays armed. Say your callsign in each clip. Play sends a clip once on its own talkgroup, so every radio and hotspot that carries that talkgroup hears it. Every service talkgroup on the master is listed; one without a recordable clip yet (for example voicemail) gets these buttons once its service can carry one.

The login ID is the device's own login, 7 to 10 digits (for a hotspot: radio ID + 2-digit ESSID). Two devices can never share one. The server mints the passphrase on Apply and shows it to you once, with a ready-to-paste Pi-Star block for a hotspot. A lost passphrase is fixed with Rotate.
