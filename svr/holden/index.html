Peers

loading…
A peer is a device logged into the master: your hotspot and the DMR PTT app during this test. Green means the master has not counted a missed keepalive for it. HBlink4 counts a miss after 30 s of silence (checked every 30 s) and drops the peer after 3, so a dead device turns yellow within about a minute and disappears within about two. This list is only live while the master's core is up; if the banner says the core is down, these rows are the last known state. An UNKNOWN peer is logged in but is not in the peer list on .151: either that list is out of date, or a device ID's passphrase has leaked.

Management

Not built yet. Enabling or disabling a peer, restarting the master, adding a peer with a one-time passphrase and viewing log lines are designed but not wired in. HBlink4 v4.8.0 has no runtime control API, so each of those actions edits the config and restarts the master, which drops every peer for a few seconds. For now, use SSH to the VPS.
